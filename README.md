Nourish – Smart Meal Planner And Recipe App

Nourish is a smart meal-planning application designed to help people maintain healthier eating habits. The app provides personalized meal suggestions based on user preferences, dietary needs, and health goals. It simplifies the process of planning balanced meals while encouraging a healthier lifestyle.

Features

🍎 Personalized Meal Suggestions
Get meal recommendations based on dietary preferences and health goals.

📅 Meal Planning
Plan daily or weekly meals easily.

🔍 Search and Explore Recipes
Discover meals from different cuisines and food categories.

❤️ Health-Focused Choices
Meals are designed to support balanced nutrition and healthy living.

🎯 Simple and Clean UI
User-friendly design that makes meal planning quick and easy.
